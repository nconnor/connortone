  <h2>How to Obtain Warranty Service</h2>

  <ul>

    <li>Claims for missing parts must be made within sixty (60) calendar days after the merchandise is received.</li>
    <li>Any claim for defective merchandise returns must be packed in original packaging.</li>

    <li>If the claim is justified, the item(s) or part(s) will be repaired or replaced, or a credit will be issued. It is our policy to replace parts whenever possible.</li>
